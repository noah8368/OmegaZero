# unc-009: H1 — position-conditional pruning margins from the head's quantile

- **Status:** H1 ACCEPTED for RFP (**+11.0 Elo**, 6130 games) — RFP site done; futility (F) next
- **Hypotheses:** [H1](../hypotheses.md#h1--position-conditional-margins-beat-a-freshly-tuned-constant-the-crux--tested-last)
  (primary — a per-position margin `Q_{1−C}(u|x)` beats a freshly-SPSA-tuned **constant** margin); also
  realizes the NPS-viability half of [H5](../hypotheses.md#h5--the-margin-is-nps-viable-via-a-folded-in-head)
  (make the quantile cheap enough for the search hot path). Builds directly on the deployed, SPRT-validated
  head from [unc-008](unc-008.md) (H6 accepted, +7.7 Elo): the same fused net that gives the mean corrector
  gives the calibrated quantiles this experiment reads.
- **Started / Concluded:** 2026-09-08 / 2026-09-10 (RFP verdict; F continues the line)
- **Code / SHA:** branch `research`; milestone commits in the Workplan table.

## Question

Does replacing a **fixed** pruning margin with the head's **position-conditional** one-sided quantile
`Q_{1−C}(u|x)` — the calibrated cp cushion "how far could the static eval be overestimating the truth here?"
— gain Elo over the *same margin re-tuned to a constant by SPSA*? Per H1 this is the crux of the whole
uncertainty-aware-search line: the honest null is that an equally-tuned constant does just as well, because
the heuristics already scale margins by depth/`improving_`. A win isolates the value of *conditioning*, not
tuning effort. Restated: does the **conditional** quantile beat the **unconditional** one at a tuned risk
level `C`?

## Design choices

### 1. RFP (reverse futility pruning) is the first margin to convert

Of every fixed-margin site (RFP, futility, razoring, NMP, LMR, singular, aspiration, qsearch delta), RFP is
first:

- **Tightest conceptual fit.** RFP (`engine.h` `ShouldReverseFutilityPrune`: at `depth ≤ 2`, non-PV,
  non-check, prune when `static_eval − depth·futility_margin ≥ beta`) trusts the static eval to be within
  `margin` of the truth **on the high side** — exactly the head's one-sided upper quantile of the eval error
  `u = v − v*`. The margin *is* `Q_{1−C}(u|x)`: large where the eval is uncertain, small where it's reliable.
- **Cleanest isolation.** One per-node decision (return beta), one margin knob → an SPRT attributes the
  result unambiguously, unlike LMR (interacts with history/`improving_`/everything) or NMP (zugzwang /
  verification subtleties).
- **Enough leverage, low risk.** Fires across the numerous `depth ≤ 2` near-leaf nodes (measurable signal),
  but shallow and bounded, so a mis-scaled margin is recoverable — not tactically catastrophic like
  aggressive deep pruning.
- **Signal already in hand.** The head forward already runs at these nodes for the H6 corrector, so the
  distribution is computed; the margin adds only the quantile read.

Baseline for the SPRT is the **freshly-SPSA-tuned constant** RFP margin (H1's honest null); `C` (the risk
level) is itself SPSA-tunable with no retraining. Later margins convert one at a time, each SPRT-gated.

#### Null baseline — concrete plan (decided 2026-09-09)

The honest-null constant RFP build **is just the parent of the quantile wiring, `8d0ff77`** — no new code.
Verified the only behavioral delta from HEAD is the RFP margin itself: at `8d0ff77` the eval is already
`Evaluate() − GetMeanCorrectionCp()` (H6 corrector + LUT present), and `cafb292`'s "v_cp breakout" was a pure
refactor (`dist.v_cp` → `Board::Evaluate()`, numerically identical mean), so the corrected eval on the
non-RFP path is unchanged across `8d0ff77 → HEAD`. RFP is the sole difference:
`static_eval − depth·futility_margin ≥ β` (null) vs `static_eval − (Q_τ(u|x) − E[u|x]) ≥ β` (test).

- **Re-tune the null, don't reuse the old value.** The mean corrector is *position-dependent*, so it reshapes
  the distribution of `static_eval` at RFP-eligible fail-high nodes — the exact quantity the margin thresholds
  against — so the optimal fixed `futility_margin` can have moved. Skipping the re-tune would leave the null
  possibly handicapped, biasing E *toward* accepting H1 (the one false-positive H1 is designed to exclude).
  (If ever pressed for compute: a zero-game diagnostic — mean of `GetMeanCorrectionCp` over RFP-eligible nodes;
  if ≲5–10 cp vs `futility_margin`, the operating point barely moved and the re-tune can be skipped *with
  evidence*. Not taken this round; re-tuning outright.)
- **Chosen the raw-`8d0ff77` null over a decoupled `RfpMargin` param.** Tradeoff accepted: `futility_margin` is
  shared with forward futility pruning, so re-tuning it nudges that heuristic too. Verdict therefore reads as
  "quantile-RFP vs re-tuned old-RFP *behavior*," not RFP margin in perfect isolation — adequate for a
  first-pass H1 read. A dedicated `RfpMargin` (≈10-line decouple) is the escalation if E is ambiguous.

**Runbook (all serial — one tune at a time under a real clock):**
1. τ tune (running: `RfpQuantile`, 8+0.08, 8 g/iter, 20k games, run `2026-09-08_20-08-58_9ea1605`) finishes →
   bake tuned `RfpQuantile` into HEAD `params.json` (the test build).
2. Check out `8d0ff77` in a worktree, build, `spsa.py run --params FutilityMargin` at the **same 8+0.08** →
   that worktree's `params.json` holds the tuned constant null.
3. E SPRT at **10+0.1**, H1 bounds: `sprt.py run --baseline-commit 8d0ff77` (makes tuned HEAD the TEST side —
   avoids the `match <BASE> <TEST>` reversal trap). Params load executable-relative, so each side reads its own
   tuned `params.json` — isolated, as in the NNUE-SPSA verification. Symmetric tuning TC (8+0.08), target-TC
   verdict (10+0.1).

### 2. Quantile primitive: LUT-accelerated MDN quantile (not naive, not std, not QR-yet)

The margin needs `Q_{1−C}` per node. Four ways to get it, and why the LUT:

- **Naive (rejected):** bisect the live mixture CDF, whose per-component Student-t CDF is an incomplete-beta
  continued fraction — measured **39,106 ns/call** (see below). At ~400k NPS a node is ~2.5 µs, so a naive
  quantile per node is a ~16× NPS crater (worse than the unc-008 float-mean crater). Unaffordable.
- **std proxy (rejected):** scaling the margin by the mixture's standard deviation is cheap but **flattens
  the heavy-tail asymmetry** — a Student-t tail is exactly what a one-sided margin must respect, and a
  symmetric std understates it. The whole point of a calibrated quantile is the fat tail.
- **QR-DQN direct-output (deferred):** train the head to emit `Q_τ` at fixed τ as output rows → quantile is
  a raw array read (fastest possible). But it needs a **retrain**, and H2 found pure QR **worst on the
  tails** — the region the margin reads. Held as an escalation (as a tail-careful hybrid) only if the LUT
  proves too slow, with P1/P3 re-validated.
- **LUT-accelerated MDN quantile (CHOSEN):** keep the deployed, validated MDN head; replace the *live*
  incomplete-beta in the per-component Student-t CDF with a **precomputed lookup table**, and bisect that.
  No retrain, keeps the exact heavy tail, cheap enough for the hot path.

**LUT design** (`TcdfLut` in `nnue.cc`): the Student-t CDF `F(t; df)` sampled on a fixed grid, bilinearly
interpolated at query time.
- Grid: `|t| ∈ [0, 32]` (1024 samples) × `df ∈ [2, 100]` (48 buckets). **Symmetry** `F(−t)=1−F(t)` halves
  the table (store `t ≥ 0`). The `df` axis is **uniform in `1/df`** — dense at small df where the heavy tail
  lives and `F` varies most, sparse as `df → normal`. ~192 KB, built once at net load (single-threaded, via
  `InitQuantileLut` in `LoadHeadStream`), read-only during search.
- `QuantileCp(tau)` bisects the LUT mixture CDF over `±32` std, **16 iterations** (~1e-3 std ≈ sub-cp
  precision). This *replaced* the naive `QuantileCp` implementation (one method, LUT-backed); `Cdf()` keeps
  the exact incomplete-beta as the validation reference.

### LUT vs naive — validated (2026-09-08)

Microbenchmark over 434 sharp+quiet positions' distributions (`unc_harness --qbench`, since removed):

| quantile method | ns/call |
|---|---|
| naive (live incomplete-beta bisection, ±50 std, 60 iters) | **39,106** |
| LUT (`QuantileCp`, ±32 std, 16 iters) | **776** |
| **speedup** | **50.4×** |

**Accuracy** (LUT vs exact incomplete-beta, per τ, max over 434 positions): ≤ **0.18 cp** at every τ from
0.01 to 0.99 (τ=0.99 tail: 0.17 cp; mean ≈0.05 cp). Essentially lossless for a margin, and the fat tail is
preserved — the reason we skipped the std proxy. The LUT is the "make the quantile NPS-viable" realization
of H5; it differs from H5's originally-decided *offline-baked cp-grain* (C51) format in that it needs no
offline baking or retrain and keeps full float precision, at the cost of a 16-iter per-node bisection rather
than a single prefix-sum — the offline grain / QR-on-grid stays the further-optimization escalation.

### RFP wiring — implementation notes (milestone C, 2026-09-08)

- **Margin.** `ShouldReverseFutilityPrune` takes the margin as an `int` (keeps `engine.h` free of the
  `UncDist` dependency); the eval site computes it. `margin = Q_{1−C}(u|x) − E[u|x]`, clamped `≥ 0`, with
  `C = params_.rfp_risk` (`RfpRisk`, a new `DblOpt`, divisor 1000, default 100 → C=0.10 → τ=0.90, bounds
  [0.01, 0.49]). SPSA/UCI-tunable with no retraining.
- **One forward, gated bisection.** At RFP-eligible nodes (`depth ≤ 2`, non-PV, non-check) a single full
  `GetUncDistribution()` yields both the mean-corrected `static_eval` (identical to the H6 mean-only value:
  same `v_cp`, same mean) and the margin. RFP can only fire when `static_eval ≥ beta` (margin ≥ 0), so the
  expensive quantile **bisection** is gated on that — computed only at fail-high frontier candidates. Every
  other node keeps the H6 int8 mean-only fast path untouched.
- **Dropped from RFP:** the old `depth·futility_margin` scaling and the `improving_` modulation — the
  conditional quantile replaces both. So the SPRT tests conditioning *and* this structural swap together;
  if ambiguous, re-add a depth/improving factor on top of the quantile.
- **NPS:** `bench_harness` microbench shows ~5–10% below the H6 build, but sequential benches on the fanless
  Air thermally throttle and are unreliable for small deltas — the **SPRT** (both engines under identical
  load) is the real NPS+Elo arbiter. Behavior verified: full-depth play + ASan soak clean.

## Metrics / pass criteria (pre-registered)

- **Primary — SPRT (Q_{1−C} RFP margin vs SPSA-tuned constant RFP margin), 10+0.1, H1 bounds.** A win = the
  *conditional* quantile beats the equally-tuned *unconditional* constant → conditioning has value, RFP
  becomes an uncertainty feature. This is the H1 crux verdict for the RFP site.
- **NPS gate:** `bench_harness` with the quantile-margin on must stay within budget of the constant-margin
  build; the LUT (776 ns/call, and RFP fires only at `depth ≤ 2`) is expected to be affordable — verified
  when wired, not assumed.
- **Calibration unchanged:** the head is the deployed unc-008 net; P1/P3 already hold. The quantile read
  changes no training.

## Interpretation / kill conditions

- **SPRT accepts H1** → RFP margin ships as `Q_{1−C}`; convert the next margin (futility) and repeat.
- **SPRT null (constant ties)** → conditioning adds no Elo at RFP *even though the head is calibrated*; the
  heuristics' existing depth/`improving_` scaling already captured the position-dependence. Try a
  higher-leverage site (futility/LMR) before concluding the margin family is a calibration study, not a
  search feature.
- **NPS crater despite the LUT** → trim bisection iters / interp, or escalate to the offline grain / QR
  deployment format (H5), then re-test.
