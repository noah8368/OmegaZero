## Question

The full 60-epoch head is well-calibrated everywhere **except its top predicted-uncertainty
decile, which under-covers** — the ν≈4 tactical tail. That tail is exactly what a pruning margin
reads (`Q_{1−C}(u|x)`, C≈0.9–0.99). Can we make the *deployed one-sided tail quantile* sharper and
better-calibrated — by (a) a tail-aware training objective and (b) post-hoc conditional conformal
recalibration — **without** degrading the bulk calibration we already have?

## Why the current fit leaves tail headroom

Training minimizes **NLL**, a density-weighted score: ~98% of `u` sits in the peak near 0, so the
gradient is dominated by the center and the rare tail is under-trained. Deployment does the
opposite — it reads a **single one-sided quantile far out in the tail**. Two consequences:

1. The objective and the deployment read are **misaligned** → the observed top-decile under-coverage.
2. The raw model quantile has **no finite-sample coverage guarantee**; split-conformal does, and
   *conditional* (Mondrian/CQR) conformal keeps the guarantee per-region.

## Setup

Frozen from unc-002: the trained `mdn_t` head, the `nnue.bin` trunk (md5 must match), the fp16
embedding cache, and `unc_11M`. **No new datagen.** Split the current **val** into a **calibration
fold** (for conformal) and a disjoint **test fold** (for all reported numbers); the train set is
untouched. Everything below is a cheap head-side change.

**Arms (2×2 over objective × recalibration):**

| | raw model quantile | + conditional conformal |
|---|---|---|
| **NLL (baseline)** | current unc-002 read | conformal-only |
| **NLL + tail term** | tail-loss-only | both |

- **Objective — tail term (added to NLL, never replacing it; H2: pure QR is worst on tails):**
  - *option A — CRPS auxiliary* on the mixture (proper, tail-sensitive; evaluate via the closed-form
    mixture CDF already used for quantiles, or a sample estimate);
  - *option B — importance-weighted NLL*, `w(u) ∝ (1 + |u|/s)^α`, up-weighting the tail;
  - *option C — auxiliary pinball at the deployed τ's*, added to NLL.
  Pick by which most improves tail-qMAE without moving bulk PIT.
- **Recalibration — conditional conformal:** on the calibration fold, compute conformity scores for
  the one-sided margin quantile (CQR-style `s = u − Q̂_τ(x)`), take their empirical (1−C)-quantile
  **per group** (Mondrian bins over `|eval|`×phase), and shift the deployed quantile by it →
  finite-sample coverage, per region. (Marginal conformal alone would restore *marginal* coverage
  but could still leave the tactical bin under-covered — the whole point is the *conditional* guarantee.)

## Metrics (on the test fold)

- **Primary:** one-sided **tail qMAE at τ ∈ {.9, .95, .99}**, and **per-region one-sided coverage
  error** (|empirical − nominal| binned by `|eval|`/phase) — the deployment-relevant numbers.
- **Guard (must not regress):** global PIT KS, central-interval coverage, bulk pinball — confirm the
  center didn't pay for the tail.
- **Downstream (the real test, later):** wire the arm's quantile into the H1 margin and SPRT — a
  sharper still-safe tail should let `C` shrink at equal risk (more pruning) or cut incorrect
  cutoffs at equal `C`.

## Guardrails

- Calibration fold **disjoint from both train and test** (conformal validity + no leakage).
- Report **conditional** coverage, not just marginal — conformal's easy win is marginal; the
  tactical-bin tail is the hard part.
- Keep NLL as the base objective; the tail term is additive and small (sweep its weight).
- The head stays foldable into the NNUE pass (H5) — conformal is a per-group scalar offset on the
  deployed grain, so it costs **nothing** at inference (a lookup adjustment), and the tail loss is
  train-time only. So unlike H12 (trunk fine-tune), unc-006 carries **no NPS bill**.

## Interpretation / kill conditions

- **Conformal helps, tail-loss doesn't** → ship conformal (free coverage guarantee), and the density
  fit's shape was already right; objective isn't the lever.
- **Tail-loss helps** → the objective mismatch was real; adopt it and re-fit.
- **Neither beats baseline on tail-qMAE/coverage** → the plain NLL fit already extracts the
  deployable tail signal (H16 falsified); the headroom is the **trunk ceiling (H12)** or **label
  noise (deeper `v*`, unc-005)**, not the head objective. Cleanly redirects effort.

## Relationship

Orthogonal to the other three "improve the distribution" levers, and cheapest of them: **H12**
(trunk fine-tune — structural, NPS-costed), **unc-005** (deeper/averaged `v*` — raises the label-noise
ceiling), and hand-built volatility features (cheap input augmentation). unc-006 touches only the
**objective + post-hoc calibration** on the *existing* embedding, so it's the first thing to try —
it says how much headroom is in the head vs. the trunk vs. the labels before spending compute on
the latter two.
