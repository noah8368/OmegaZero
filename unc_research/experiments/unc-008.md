# unc-008: H6 corrector-swap — model mean replaces correction history

- **Status:** in progress (started 2026-09-07)
- **Hypotheses:** [H6](../hypotheses.md#h6) (primary — the corrector-swap); enabled by
  [unc-007](unc-007.md) P2 (E[u|x] is a useful, calibrated eval corrector: r=0.43, R²=0.19,
  MAE −9.6% vs raw). Also tests **[H5](../hypotheses.md#h5)-B** here — the head forward must be made
  NPS-cheap so a naive-float NPS crater does not confound (or silently sink) the SPRT vs `main`. The
  corrector uses only the head's **mean** (an MLP forward + weighted sum — no quantile inversion), so
  H5-B for unc-008 is "make the mean forward cheap"; the grain-quantized *quantile* head is deferred to
  H1/unc-004.
- **Started / Concluded:** 2026-09-07 / —
- **Code / SHA:** branch `research`; milestone commits tracked in the Workplan table below.

## Question

Does replacing the engine's **online pawn-hash correction history** (`GetCorrectedEval`) with the
uncertainty head's **conditional mean** `E[u|x]` — used as `corrected_eval = raw_eval − E[u|x]` — gain
Elo, measured by an SPRT of this branch's head vs `main`? unc-007 showed the mean beats *raw* eval by
~10%; corr-hist also beats raw (that is its job), so the open question is **model-mean vs corr-hist head
to head, net of the head's NPS cost.**

## The architectural decision (settled by Noah, 2026-09-07)

Before the swap, collapse the engine to **one eval code path**. The dual/fallback paths (HCE,
NNUE-only-vs-fused, `has_head_` degradation) are retired: **the full fused net (`nnue_unc.bin`) is
required from here on out**, and the engine reads it by default. Rationale: the corrected eval now
*always* consumes the head, so a code path without a head is dead weight and a source of bugs. This
realizes the "future direction" noted during unc-007 (drop the OZNN-only branch and `has_head_` guards;
`nnue.bin`/the deployed net just *is* an OZNU).

- **Strip HCE entirely.** `Board::Evaluate()` becomes NNUE-only; remove `--hce`, `hce_mode`, the "hce"
  params profile, `ProfileForEvalMode`'s branch, and the handcrafted eval helpers in `board.cc`. Corr-hist
  (`GetCorrectedEval`/`UpdateCorrectionHistory`) is NOT HCE — it stays until Phase F replaces it.
- **Fused net required + default.** Default net path → the deployed `nnue_unc.bin`; a missing/incomplete
  net is **fatal** (no fallback). The loader still parses a bare OZNN section (that is how the embedded
  trunk loads), but the play/UCI engine requires a head.
- **Clean up.** Remove now-dead `IsLoaded()`-else-HCE branches and the `has_head_` degradation in the
  play path.

## Setup / phases

Signed error, raw-eval target, deep-`v*` labels — all inherited from unc-002/unc-007; the trained head is
`models/unc_head/2026-09-06_00-58-01_nnue_10634559pos` fused into `nnue_unc.bin`. Only the eval's
*correction term* changes.

**Intermediate diagnostic (before the swap SPRT):** during self-play — where corr-hist is *live* — log,
per eval, the error-vs-`v*` of all three correctors (raw, corr-hist-corrected, model-mean-corrected).
This is the one comparison unc-007 could not make (val positions are static; corr-hist is path-dependent).
If model-mean does not beat corr-hist here, we learn it cheaply and keep corr-hist (head → margins only).

## Eval speed / NPS (H5-B) — making the head forward cheap

The head currently runs a **float** MLP (`512→128→128→20`, ~84k MACs) off the shared clamped-accumulator
concat (the embedding itself is free — H5). For the **corrector (H6) only the mean matters**: `E[u|x] =
Σ πₖ μₖ` de-standardized, which needs the MLP forward + a softmax + a weighted sum — **no bisection, no
incomplete-beta, no float `exp` in the tail**. So the H6 NPS cost is just the MLP forward, and the levers,
in priority order, are:

1. **int8-quantize the head MLP** like the trunk (weights int8, clamped int8 activations) so its ~84k MACs
   ride the same integer path; on the M4 (NEON `SDOT`/`UDOT`) that is well under a microsecond. *(primary
   H5-B lever for unc-008.)*
2. **Branch late off a shared hidden layer.** The `512→128` first layer is 65k of the 84k MACs. Conditioning
   the head off an *existing* small activation (e.g. the 32-dim L2 output the eval already computes) makes it
   `32→h`, ~16× cheaper — at the cost of information vs the 512 embedding, so it is an **ablation** (does the
   cheaper-conditioned mean stay a good corrector? re-run the unc-007 P2 MAE-reduction check). Only if Tier 1
   is insufficient.
3. **Compute the mean only where used.** With H6 the mean rides every eval, but softmax/df/σ heads are
   unused for the mean — compute only `π` and `μ` (skip `log_sigma`, `log_df` outputs) for the corrector.
4. **Distilled mean head** — a tiny separate head that outputs `E[u|x]` directly (one scalar), trained to
   match the MDN mean; the cheapest possible, if 1–3 still cost too much.
5. **(Base eval, independent)** the trunk `ForwardFromAccumulators` is scalar today (`-march=native -O3`
   may auto-vectorize the int8→int32 dot, not guaranteed); hand-NEON it if `bench_harness` fingers the eval
   as the bottleneck — a general NPS win, but **profile first**.

Also note the **quantile grid (full H5-B)** — bake the distribution onto a fixed cp grid so `Q_{1−C}` is an
integer prefix-sum+threshold (C51/QR-DQN trick), removing the ~9k continued-fraction steps the *quantile*
costs — is **deferred to H1/unc-004**, which is what actually reads quantiles at search time. unc-008 does
not need it.

**Gate:** `bench_harness` NPS with the corrector on must land within a pre-set budget of `main`'s NPS (the
SPRT then measures net Elo = eval gain − residual NPS cost). Report NPS before the swap, after the float
swap, and after int8 — so a crater is *visible and attributed*, never silent.

## Metrics / pass criteria (pre-registered)

- **Diagnostic gate:** model-mean-corrected MAE/RMSE vs `v*` ≤ corr-hist-corrected, over a self-play sample
  (in-distribution). A clear win green-lights the SPRT; a loss triggers the fallback (residual corr-hist).
- **Primary — SPRT (this branch head vs `main`).** After an SPSA re-tune of the search params under the new
  eval, run an SPRT at a real TC (10+0.1). H1-style bounds. This is the H6 verdict: does the model-mean
  corrector (net of its NPS cost) gain Elo over `main` (corr-hist).
- **NPS report:** `bench_harness` NPS before/after the swap — the head forward per eval is the cost H5
  flags; the SPRT measures whether the eval gain outweighs it.

## Results

**E — three-corrector self-play diagnostic (2026-09-07, n=1144, 48 games, st=0.1, depth-12 `v*`).**
`datagen.cc` gained a `corrector` mode (superset of uncertainty mode: loads the fused net, logs
`corrhist = GetCorrectedEval(v)` and `model_mean = v − E[u|x]` per sampled position);
`unc_research/scripts/unc008_corrector_diag.py` analyzes + plots. Error vs deep-search `v*`:

| corrector | MAE (cp) | RMSE (cp) | vs raw |
|---|---|---|---|
| raw eval | 115.6 | 185.3 | — |
| **model-mean** (`v − E[u\|x]`) | **106.5** | **166.4** | **−7.9%** |
| corr-hist (`GetCorrectedEval`) | 140.4 | 205.5 | **+21.5% (worse)** |

Model-mean beats corr-hist head-to-head — better on **62.8%** of positions, Wilcoxon **p=1.4e-21** — and
beats raw by ~8% (consistent with unc-007 P2's ~10% on val). **So the mean is a genuine deep-truth
corrector; corr-hist is not, in this regime.**

**Honest caveat (regime).** corr-hist coming out *worse than raw* is a flag that this diagnostic's
conditions disadvantage it: corr-hist is trained *online from the game's own searches at play depth*
(here a shallow `st=0.1`, so noisy signal that visibly over-corrects some positions), and it targets the
*play-depth* search score — **not** the depth-12 `v*` this metric scores against. model-mean, by
contrast, was trained precisely to predict depth-12 `v*` error, so it has a home-target advantage here.
The eval's true job is to estimate deep-search truth, so model-mean winning on the `v*` target is the
relevant signal — but this is a *pre-check*, not the verdict. The decision is the **SPRT (Phase I)** at a
real TC, where corr-hist operates in its normal (deeper-search) regime. **E green-lights building Phase F**
(the mean is at worst competitive and likely better), with the H6 judgment reserved for the SPRT.

**F — model-mean corrector wired into the live search path + naive-float NPS crater (2026-09-07).**
`Pvs`'s static-eval site now computes `corrected = raw − E[u|x]` from a single fused forward off the
shared accumulators (`dist.v_cp − round(dist.MeanCp())`; `dist.v_cp == Board::Evaluate()`, so no extra
trunk pass), and the `UpdateCorrectionHistory` call is removed from the search path. Corr-hist tables /
`GetCorrectedEval` / `UpdateCorrectionHistory` are **kept but dormant** — still referenced by the datagen
corrector diagnostic and reserved for the pre-registered residual-corrhist fallback; physical deletion
waits on the SPRT (Phase I) verdict. `bench_harness` and `debug_harness` now load the **fused** net so the
head forward is actually exercised (bench would otherwise hide the crater; the ASan soak now covers the new
eval path).

NPS (`bench_harness 4`, same machine/positions), naive **float** MLP head (`512→128→128→20`, ~84k MACs)
recomputed per non-check node:

| position | before (corr-hist, headless) | after (model-mean, fused) | slowdown |
|---|---|---|---|
| opening | 484,468 | 57,007 | 8.5× |
| midgame | 441,836 | 60,945 | 7.2× |
| kiwipete | 217,677 | 66,823 | 3.3× |
| endgame | 408,948 | 57,213 | 7.1× |
| **average** | **388,017** | **60,510** | **6.4× (−84%)** |

**The crater is severe and fully attributed to the per-eval float head forward** (the head runs on every
non-check node; qsearch stand-pat stays raw, which is why the tactical kiwipete — heavy qsearch — craters
least). A 6.4× NPS loss would swamp any eval-quality gain in an SPRT, so **Phase G (H5-B: int8-quantize +
compute only π/μ) is mandatory before Phase H/I** — this number is the "naive-float NPS crater" Phase F was
pre-registered to record, now visible and quantified rather than silently sunk.

## Interpretation / kill conditions

- **Diagnostic loss** (model-mean worse than corr-hist on self-play error) → keep corr-hist; the frozen
  offline mean does not beat online adaptivity for the mean. Head is retained for **margins** (H1) only.
- **SPRT H1_ACCEPTED** → ship the corrector-swap; corr-hist removed, its job absorbed by the head (the
  "distributional generalization of correction history" realized). **Fallback if borderline/regressing:**
  keep a small residual online corr-hist on top of the model-mean baseline (best of both), re-SPRT.
- **SPRT regresses despite a good diagnostic** → the NPS cost of the per-eval head forward outweighs the
  eval gain (H5 kill condition); needs the cheaper head first (int8 + late-branch / distilled mean, see
  unc-007 "inference speed" notes) before re-testing.
